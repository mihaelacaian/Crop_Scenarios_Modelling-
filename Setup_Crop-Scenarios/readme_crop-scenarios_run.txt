Set-up for climate-crop scenarios under various management practices; it uses:
  - input climate data from the output of "Preproc_Clim_to_crop" (*WTH file)
  - set-up user-choice for climate scenario, time-interval, region (SOIL.txt), genotype
  - set-up user-choice for management practice (default has 12 planting dates x 3 fertilization levels)
